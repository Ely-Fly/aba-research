Parent-Mediated Early Intervention in Infants and Toddlers at Elevated Likelihood for Autism: A Systematic Review of Randomized Controlled Trials

Conti et al.  ·  Frontiers in Psychiatry  ·  April 13, 2026

This systematic review of 11 RCTs found that parent-mediated interventions for infants under 18 months improved communication in 4 studies, reduced autism symptom severity in 4, and enhanced parent-infant interactions in 6 — providing the strongest available evidence for intervening before a formal diagnosis. Approaches evaluated include iBASIS-VIPP, Baby JASPER, and ImPACT, all emphasizing caregiver coaching as the delivery mechanism.

Clinically Significant Outcomes of Early Intensive Behavioral Intervention for Children with Autism Spectrum Disorders: An Individual Participant Data Meta-Analysis

Eldevik et al.  ·  Autism Research  ·  January 2026

This rigorous IPD meta-analysis of 15 EIBI studies (621 children) found large effect sizes for adaptive behavior (0.66), intellectual functioning (0.87), and autism severity reduction (1.36), with a number needed to treat of 4–7. Treatment intensity was the strongest consistent predictor of outcomes, leading the authors to recommend EIBI as the preferred treatment for young children with ASD.

Early Start Denver Model Effectiveness in Young Autistic Children: A Large Multicentric Randomised Controlled Trial in Two European Countries

Geoffray et al.  ·  BMJ Mental Health  ·  June 5, 2025

This landmark RCT of 180 toddlers aged 19–36 months found that adding ESDM to treatment-as-usual did not significantly improve developmental quotient over 2 years (p=0.14), challenging universal recommendations for ESDM across all toddlers. Notably, 38% of the ESDM group vs. 24% of controls achieved 2+ years of developmental growth, suggesting meaningful benefit for specific subgroups worth identifying in clinical practice.

The Impact of Early Intensive Behavioral and Developmental Interventions on Key Developmental Outcomes in Young Children with Autism Spectrum Disorder: A Narrative Review

Avula et al.  ·  Cureus  ·  September 11, 2025

This narrative review synthesized evidence for EIBI, ESDM, and PRT, finding IQ gains of 9–15 points and significant language development improvements, with ESDM showing particularly comprehensive developmental effects across domains. The authors found that lower-intensity models (5–15 hours/week) can match comprehensive programs for targeted outcomes — an important finding for clinicians navigating access and service hour constraints.

Evidence-Based Interventions in Autism

Anixt, Ehrhardt & Duncan  ·  Pediatric Clinics of North America  ·  January 30, 2024

This clinically-focused review affirms that interventions started before age 3 — when brain plasticity is highest — yield the greatest long-term gains in social communication, cognition, and adaptive skills, and provides a practical framework for connecting families to federally mandated Part C services. It reviews EIBI, ESDM, and naturalistic developmental behavioral interventions with guidance on model selection suited to individual child profiles.
