# My ABA Research Requirements

## Who I am
BCBA working in a pediatric clinic with kids ages 0–10.

## Topics I care about most
- Early intervention (ages 0–6)
- Verbal behavior and AAC (Augmentative and Alternative Communication)
- BACB ethics and supervision updates
- Medicaid billing changes in Virginia
- New medications and therapies for autism/ABA

## Sources I want
- PubMed (always include — prioritize peer-reviewed studies)
- BACB.com (for ethics, supervision, and certification updates)
- Autism Speaks (autismspeaks.org)
- Physicians and researchers posting on X (Twitter) — credentialed accounts only (MD, PhD, BCBA)
- Skip general news blogs, opinion sites, and non-credentialed sources

## Output format I prefer
- Output must be a downloadable, well-formatted HTML file
- Top 5 most relevant results only (do not exceed 5)
- The HTML should look clean and professional — suitable for sharing with colleagues
- Include a header with report title, date generated, and topic searched
- For each result, include:
  - **Title** (as a clickable link to the article/paper)
  - **Source** (publication or website name)
  - **Date** (must be within last 3 years)
  - **2-sentence summary** (plain language, clinically relevant)
  - A colored topic tag/badge (e.g. "Early Intervention", "AAC", "Medicaid", "Ethics", "Medication")
- Use cards or sections to visually separate each result
- Clean typography, good spacing, easy to read and print
- No synthesis paragraph needed
- No long introductions — go straight to results

## Things to exclude
- Anything published more than 3 years ago
- Opinion pieces or parent blogs
- Non-peer-reviewed commentary unless from BACB or major org
- General news sites (WebMD, Healthline, Psychology Today blogs)
